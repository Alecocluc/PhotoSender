# Third-party notices

Pherry ships these third-party assets inside both apps. Nothing is fetched at runtime.

## Archivo

Typeface by Omnibus-Type. Used for all running text, headings and the wordmark.
Files: `app/src/main/res/font/archivo.ttf`, `desktop/renderer/fonts/Archivo.ttf`.
Licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; the full text is in `desktop/renderer/fonts/OFL-Archivo.txt`.

## Martian Mono

Typeface by Evil Martians. Used for edge print, codes and measurements.
Files: `app/src/main/res/font/martian_mono.ttf`, `desktop/renderer/fonts/MartianMono.ttf`.
Licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; the full text is in `desktop/renderer/fonts/OFL-MartianMono.txt`.
